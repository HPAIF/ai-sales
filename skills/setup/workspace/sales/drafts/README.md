# Outreach drafts

One file per message or short sequence: `OD-XXXX-short-slug.md`, from the plugin's
`templates/outreach-draft.md`. Front-matter `status:` drives the session hook:

- `draft` — being written, or blocked on a fact, a claim or a lawyer check
- `ready` — waiting for the founder's approval
- `approved` — the founder approved the text; **the founder sends it**
- `sent` — the founder said it was sent (date and channel recorded)
- `dropped` — not used

The AI sales member never sends, schedules or posts a message. Never delete a draft; change its
status.
